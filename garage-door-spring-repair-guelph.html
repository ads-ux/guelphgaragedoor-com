Why Garage Door Springs Break in Guelph

Springs do the heavy lifting — the opener only guides the door. Every open and close is one cycle, and a standard spring is rated for about 10,000 of them. For a family using the garage two or three times a day, that is seven to ten years. Guelph winters shorten it: cold steel is more brittle, which is why so many springs snap on the first hard-frost mornings of November and December.

When a spring breaks, the door suddenly weighs 70 to 150 kilograms with nothing counterbalancing it. Running the opener against that weight can strip the drive gear or snap a lift cable, turning one repair into three. If you hear a bang and the door will not lift, disconnect the opener and call.

Spring trouble is just one kind of failure — for openers, cables, tracks and panels see our garage door repair services in Guelph. And if the door is over 20 years old and breaking repeatedly, it may be time to look at garage door installation instead.
